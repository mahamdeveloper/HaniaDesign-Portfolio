Logo & branding designer — helping businesses tell their story visually.

Hania Designs is a graphic design practice specializing in logo design, brand identity, social media design, and visual communication for businesses.

The work centers on giving a business a visual identity it can use consistently — a logo that holds up across formats, a social media presence that looks considered, and print and digital collateral that carries the same voice throughout.

Recent projects have covered branding and marketing design for a marble & stone supplier, document and template design for a document management services company, and social media design for a school.
